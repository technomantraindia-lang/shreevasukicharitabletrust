Programs

How we help every day

Daily Care & Support

Round-the-clock personal care, health monitoring and companionship for every elder.

Nutritious Meals

Thousands of fresh meals served daily in hospitals, streets and our old age home.

Community Outreach

Regular seva drives that take food, clothing and care to those who need it most.

Education for All

Scholarships, supplies and guidance that keep underprivileged students learning.

Compassion in Action

Gau Seva and celebration sevas that spread kindness on every meaningful occasion.

01 — Shelter

A home, not a hostel

At Vatsalya Vrudhashram, every thali is served with respect. Elders eat in peace, rest with dignity, and are never alone.

02 — Nourish

Meals where they matter

From hospital courtyards to roadside queues, our volunteers serve fresh food to patients, families, and anyone who is hungry.

03 — Streets

No one waits hungry

Sudama nu Rasodu and our street seva keep the kitchen open — day and night — for those the city overlooks.

04 — Gau Seva

Care for every being

Rescued cows receive shelter, feed, and gentle hands. Seva does not stop at the human heart.

05 — Future

Education as seva

Fees, books, and festivals of learning — so a child’s tomorrow is not decided by today’s hardship.

06 — Celebrate

Joy is also seva

Birthdays, anniversaries, and festivals are shared with our elders. Your occasion can become their smile.

Moments of impact

Real photographs from our home, kitchens, streets, and celebrations.

Special Services & Celebrations

On birthdays, marriage anniversaries, Shravan, Adhik month, Agiyaras, and other festivals, celebrate with the elders at our old age home by sponsoring meals and sharing joy with them.
