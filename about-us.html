OUR STORY

A Journey of
Faith, Love & Service

Founded on the timeless values of compassion and selfless service, Shree Vasuki Trust began as a humble effort to help stray animals and people in distress. Over the years, with the blessings of our community and supporters, we have grown into a movement of hope.

Inspired by the teachings of love, service and humanity.
From feeding the hungry to caring for voiceless animals.
Empowering communities through health, education and support programs.
Every small act, when done with love, creates a ripple of change.
