Vatsalya Group

Together in Support. Together in Seva.

Vatsalya Group is a community-based social support and charitable initiative created with the spirit of Seva, Humanity and Mutual Support. Our purpose is simple: members support one another during the most difficult moments of life, while a portion of every contribution is also dedicated to meaningful charitable activities such as cow feeding, animal welfare, support for the visually impaired and other social causes.

How the Vatsalya Group System Works

A simple, transparent and community-driven support model.

1

Become a Registered Member

To become part of Vatsalya Group, each person completes the registration process and contributes:

One-Time Registration Fee
₹400
Member Contribution
₹1,100
The registration fee is separate from the member support contribution. Every registered member becomes part of the Vatsalya Group support network.
2

Members Create a Support Pool

The ₹1,100 contribution collected from every participating member forms a common support pool.

11 Members × ₹1,100
= ₹12,100
This ₹12,100 becomes the support amount available under the Vatsalya Group system.
3

Support in Case of a Member's Death

In the unfortunate event of the death of any registered member, irrespective of the situation and subject to verification and the rules of Vatsalya Group, the collected support fund will be distributed as follows:

80%
Family Support
80% of the collected amount will be provided as financial assistance to the eligible family / nominee of the deceased member.
From ₹12,100:
80% = ₹9,680
10%
Social & Charitable Seva
  • Gau Seva / Cow Feeding
  • Animal Feeding & Welfare
  • Support for Andhjan Mandal / Visually Impaired Person
  • Other genuine humanitarian activities approved by Vatsalya Group
From ₹12,100:
10% = ₹1,210
10%
Reserve Fund
The remaining 10% will be deposited into the designated Vatsalya Group bank account / reserve fund. This reserve can help strengthen the organization and support administration and approved future welfare Group's rules and policy.
From ₹12,100:
10% = ₹1,210

Simple Example

Group of 11 Members
👥 11 × ₹1,100 = ₹12,100
🟢 ₹9,680 80% Family Support
🟠 ₹1,210 10% Charitable Seva
🔵 ₹1,210 10% Group Reserve Fund
Total = ₹12,100
4

Contribution Is Renewed After Support Is Provided

Continuous cycle of mutual assistance

After support has been provided following the death of a registered member, the remaining active members will contribute ₹1,100 again to rebuild the support pool.

Initially there are 11 members. If one member passes away and support is provided, the remaining 10 active members will make the next contribution of ₹1,100 each, subject to the membership rules.

This helps the Group remain prepared to support another member's family whenever such an unfortunate situation arises.

Membership Continuation & Contribution Policy

To keep the Vatsalya Group support system active and fair for all members, every registered member must make the required contribution of ₹1,100 whenever a contribution cycle is announced after the death of an eligible member.

If any member:

  • Fails to pay the required ₹1,100 contribution within the prescribed time, or
  • Refuses to make the contribution after an eligible member's death, or
  • Does not wish to continue participating in the support cycle,

then that person's Vatsalya Group membership will be considered terminated from that day.

Once the membership is terminated, the person will no longer be considered an active member and will not be eligible for future benefits or financial assistance under the Vatsalya Group support system.

Any future rejoining, if permitted, will be subject to the prevailing registration process, membership rules, applicable fees and approval of Vatsalya Group.

Important: Every member must understand that the Vatsalya Group system works through mutual and timely participation. Regular contribution is essential to keep the support cycle active for every member.

વાત્સલ્ય ગ્રુપની સહાય વ્યવસ્થા દરેક સભ્ય માટે ન્યાયસંગત અને સતત કાર્યરત રહે તે માટે, કોઈ પાત્ર સભ્યના અવસાન બાદ જ્યારે નવું યોગદાન શરૂ કરવામાં આવે ત્યારે દરેક સક્રિય સભ્યે ₹૧,૧૦૦નું ફરજિયાત યોગદાન આપવાનું રહેશે.

જો કોઈ સભ્ય:

  • નક્કી કરેલી સમયમર્યાદામાં ₹૧,૧૦૦નું યોગદાન આપવામાં નિષ્ફળ જાય, અથવા
  • કોઈ પાત્ર સભ્યના અવસાન બાદ જરૂરી યોગદાન આપવાનો ઈનકાર કરે, અથવા
  • આગળની સહાય વ્યવસ્થામાં જોડાઈ રહેવા ઇચ્છતો ન હોય,

તો તે વ્યક્તિનું વાત્સલ્ય ગ્રુપનું સભ્યપદ તે જ દિવસથી સમાપ્ત માનવામાં આવશે.

સભ્યપદ સમાપ્ત થયા બાદ તે વ્યક્તિને સક્રિય સભ્ય તરીકે ગણવામાં આવશે નહીં અને તેને વાત્સલ્ય ગ્રુપની ભવિષ્યની સહાય અથવા આર્થિક લાભ માટે પાત્ર માનવામાં આવશે નહીં.

જો ભવિષ્યમાં ફરીથી સભ્ય તરીકે જોડાવાની મંજૂરી આપવામાં આવે, તો તે સમયના લાગુ પડતા નોંધણી નિયમો, ફી, સભ્યપદની શરતો અને વાત્સલ્ય ગ્રુપની મંજૂરી મુજબ ફરી નોંધણી કરાવવી પડશે.

મહત્વપૂર્ણ: વાત્સલ્ય ગ્રુપની વ્યવસ્થા પરસ્પર સહકાર અને સમયસર યોગદાનના આધાર પર કાર્ય કરે છે. દરેક સભ્યનું સમયસર યોગદાન સમગ્ર સહાય ચક્ર ચાલુ રાખવા માટે અત્યંત જરૂરી છે.
